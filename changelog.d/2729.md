### Changes

- `tehas status --json` has an `analytics` field, `on` or `off`, from `TEHAS_ANALYTICS` of the instance, so a program asks whether the factory records its runs instead of running `tehas analytics copy` and reading why it ended 78 (#2729).

### Adopter action

A program that skips the copy when it ends 78 and says analytics is off can read the field instead, and take every 78 of the copy as a fault of the settings. `api/status.schema.json` and the six samples of the object beside it hold the field.
