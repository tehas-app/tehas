### Changes

- The health check's `github` names its last failed call in tehas's words, one fixed line per kind of failure (`GitHub failed with a server error`, `GitHub could not be reached`, ...), not gh's text. `tehas status` shows the same line. The log of the poll, `tehasbot status` and tidy still quote gh's words.

### Adopter action

A program that reads `checks.github.last` in `live/health.json` reads the new lines. The lines written before the update stay until they leave the ten-minute window.
