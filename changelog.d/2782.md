### Changes

- `tehas answer <issue> <choice> --by <login> [--json]` answers the question a stopped ticket or a planning session waits on, for a person, from a program on the host such as an industry: `retry`, `retry-text` or `park` for a stop, `option:<k>`, `update`, `end` or `--text <file>` for a planning question. `--by` must be an account of `TEHAS_ALLOWED_LABELERS`, or it stops with 78. The answer is recorded in the state store with who gave it and when, and the next poll acts on it as on a reply of that account that names the same choice (#2782).
- `tehas answer <issue> --text - --by <login>` reads the reply in words from standard input, for a program that may not write a file on the host. It is bound to 1 MiB, as a file is, and empty input stops with 64 (#2782).
- `tehas api --json` lists the feature word `answer` in `features`: a program that manages factories of more than one release checks for it before it uses `tehas answer` (#2782).
- `tehas question <issue> [--json]` prints the question an issue waits on and its choices, each in the words `tehas answer` takes, so a program can show them and answer one. `tehas api --json` lists the feature word `question`. The store is at schema 25: a stop's question keeps the choices it was asked with (#2782).
- A retry an answer asked for is recorded with `how` `answer` in `tehas history`, the start comment and the approval record (#2782).
- The state store is at schema 24: it adds `answers`, the answers `tehas answer` gave, each bound to its question, and a cycle's `how` may say `answer` (#2782).

- The tick boxes are gone. A stopped cycle's comment and a planning question list their choices as plain lines, each with what it leads to and ending in a full stop, and ask a reply: a stop's by the word of a choice it offers (`retry`, `retry-text`, `park`), a planning question's by the number of one of the planner's options, by `end`, or in your own words. A bare number answers a planning question with no options of its own in words. A stop that offers no retry, a second develop timeout on the same text, asks nothing: its comment says to split the issue or edit the body. Nothing reads a tick any more: a box ticked in a comment of before 2.0 does nothing at all. Answer a stop by a reply from an allowed account or through `tehas answer`. A stop's reply stands until it is acted on, declined by the factory's note, or replaced by a newer reply that chooses. A newer reply that names a choice the stop does not offer starts nothing, and the older choice does not act either: the factory answers once that it is not offered. A retry a reply answered is recorded with `how` `reply`, one `tehas answer` gave with `how` `answer`. The park's hold now says "edit the body, or reply to the park comment with the word of one of its retries" in `tehas status`, `tehas blockers` and `held.clear`, and a park that offered no retry is held with "edit the body, or split the issue with `tehas:plan`" (#2782).
- The state store is at schema 26: a cycle's `how` may say `reply` (#2782).

### Adopter action

Run `tehas update`, which migrates the store to schema 26. Answer a stopped cycle or a planning question by a reply in a comment, or through `tehas answer`: ticking a box does nothing now. A program that ticked boxes for a person moves to `tehas answer`, and reads the choices with `tehas question`. A program that counts retries by `how` reads `reply` beside `answer`.
