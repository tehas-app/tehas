### Changes

- A factory can turn outsource on. With `TEHAS_OUTSOURCE=1` and the
  repositories of `TEHAS_OUTSOURCE_REPOS`, its state store holds the
  contractors, the jobs, each issue's outsourced attempts and the one-time
  invite codes, and the store answers the verbs that move a job through
  `offered`, `running`, `stopping`, `finished`, `released` and `lost`, with a
  row of history for every change. A job whose contractor goes quiet past
  `TEHAS_OUTSOURCE_TIMEOUT` is lost `TEHAS_OUTSOURCE_GRACE` seconds later, a
  contractor that lost one is given no new job for `TEHAS_OUTSOURCE_REST`
  seconds, and an issue that lost `TEHAS_OUTSOURCE_RETRIES` of them runs
  in-house. `store init --outsource` and `store migrate --outsource` lay the
  five tables; a factory that leaves `TEHAS_OUTSOURCE` at 0 has no table of the
  feature at all, and every verb of it is refused there. Nothing sends a job to
  a contractor yet: no installed command calls these verbs, so turning the
  setting on changes nothing about what the factory does.
  [The settings table](/tools#instance-settings) and
  [Features](/features) say what is built.

### Adopter action

`store init --outsource` and `store migrate --outsource` take the new flag, so
a caller that has loaded the instance's settings passes it when
`TEHAS_OUTSOURCE` is 1; `install.sh` and `tehas update` do not pass it yet, and
an instance that turns the setting on before they do gets the tables at the
next release that carries them. To turn the feature on, add to `instance.env`:

```
TEHAS_OUTSOURCE=1
TEHAS_OUTSOURCE_REPOS=acme/widget
```

The other settings of the feature keep their defaults
(`TEHAS_OUTSOURCE_TIMEOUT`, `_GRACE`, `_RETRIES`, `_REST`,
`_JOIN_TTL`); `instances/example/optional.env` lists them with those defaults.
