### Changes

- A reserve that is no size is named as what it is. The dispatcher's hold says
  `TEHAS_CYCLE_RESERVE=<word> is not a size` instead of "the slice was not
  probed", so the line points at `instance.env` and not at
  `/sys/fs/cgroup/tehas.slice`. `console slice room` prints the setting's own
  word in its reserve field where a size is printed in bytes, and `nosize` when
  the name is unset, so the line keeps its places. A pressure figure the reader
  cannot make a number of counts as the slice stalling, not as a slice that
  never stalled (#2235).
