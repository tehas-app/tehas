### Changes

- A poll that ends a planning session asks for the next poll at once, as a cycle's end does, and writes `live/demand.json` again at its end with the issue the session held counted. A ready issue no longer waits two timer polls after its session ends (#2708).
- `tehas limit set` and `tehas limit clear` ask for a poll when they let more cycles run while the last poll's demand has an issue waiting, so the raised limit is acted on now and not at the next timer (#2708).
