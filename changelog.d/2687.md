### Changes

- The product wiki is registered as a feature still being built, `wiki`, with its switch `TEHAS_WIKI`. A release refuses `TEHAS_WIKI=on` with 78. With it on in a test or a lab build, `tehas wiki [--json]` says which wiki build is current and how far it is behind main, in commits and in days or months, and `tehasbot wiki` writes the same to `~/tehas/live/wiki.json` every five minutes. Nothing builds a wiki yet (#2687).
