tehas work: one pi-rukas /work cycle, run headless in the sandbox image, to
its end. The cycle is `tehas-cycle work` (console/cycle/src/work.rs), which
says what the container is given and what every exit status means.

The console loads the settings and execs it. It takes
state/cycles/<issue>.lock and hands it down to every program of the cycle,
the docker client among them. Under tehasbot cycle the lock comes down
already held, and is taken as it is.

Usage: tehas work <issue-number> [--restart] [--driver lean|classic]
The driver is the approval's, or classic; --driver wins over it.

Exit: 64 the argument is not an issue number, or tehas-cycle refuses the
options after it; 78 the settings are missing or malformed, or there is no
tehas-cycle beside the console; 5 a cycle for this issue is running, nothing
was touched; else tehas-cycle work's.
