tehas analytics: the factory's 365 days of runs, for a person or a program.

The factory records its runs, their steps and roles, issue events and poll
samples in ~/tehas/state/analytics.db, and keeps 365 days of them. This
command reads them through ~/tehas/libexec/analytics, the program that holds
all of their SQL.

Usage:
  tehas analytics report               the ten-day report, for a person
  tehas analytics export <file> [--json]
                                       a consistent copy of the database

report prints what the dashboard's "Last 10 days" shows, as text.

export writes the copy to <file>, mode 600, and never replaces a file. With
--json it then prints, read from the copy:
  {"api":1,"schema":1,"from":1792022400,"to":1792800000,"runs":42}
schema is the copy's schema version, from and to the oldest start and the
newest end of its runs, in Unix seconds, both left out when it has none.
It takes no lock a cycle holds and makes no network call.

Exit: 0 printed or written; 64 a bad argument; 69 analytics is off
(TEHAS_ANALYTICS=off) or the program is not installed, which writes nothing;
70 the database could not be read; 73 <file> exists or cannot be made; 78
the instance settings are missing or malformed.
