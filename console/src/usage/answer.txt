tehas answer <issue> <choice>: answer the question an issue waits on, for a person.

A program on the host, such as an industry or a chat a person answers in,
gives here the answer of a person to the question a stopped ticket or a
planning session waits on, beside a reply on the issue.
--by names the person. It is trusted as the host's caller, as tehas start
trusts its caller, and it must be an account that may authorise work for
this factory: one of TEHAS_ALLOWED_LABELERS.

The answer is recorded in the state store (state/tehas.db), bound to the
question on record of the issue, with who gave it, when, and how (`answer`).
Nothing happens at once. The next poll acts on it as on a reply of that
account that names the same choice, under the rules every answer passes: a retry waits for room
and starts only while the issue is still stopped and its text unchanged. An
answer that cannot be taken is taken away, and the poll says why on the
issue. Running it again before the poll replaces the answer that waits. A
question answered, or a new question asked, leaves an answer to the old one
with nothing to answer.

Usage:
  tehas answer <issue> <choice> --by <login>        answer with a choice
  tehas answer <issue> --text <file> --by <login>   answer a planning question in words
  tehas answer <issue> --text - --by <login>        the same, the words read from standard input
  tehas answer ... --json                           answer with what was recorded

  <choice>  for a stopped ticket:
              retry          start over with the issue and its comments
              retry-text     start over with the issue text only
              park           leave it stopped
            for a planning question:
              option:<k>     the option numbered <k>, from 1, as the question lists it
              update         the option "Update this issue"
              end            end the planning session
  --text    a reply in words to a planning question, read from <file>, or
            from standard input with `-`, for a caller that may not write a
            file on the host. Either is at most 1 MiB, and stdin with no
            words is a bad argument

Each command prints one line:

  answered the park question of #12 for alice at 2026-10-17T14:00:20Z: retry; the next poll acts on it

With --json, {"api": 1, "issue": 12, "question": {"id": 4001, "kind":
"park"}, "answer": {"choice": "retry", "option": null, "by": "alice", "how":
"answer", "at": "..."}, "replaced": false}.

Exit: 0 recorded; 1 the state store refused or is not installed; 3 no
question of that kind waits on the issue, or the question does not offer
that option; 64 a bad argument, which writes nothing; 78 --by may not
authorise work, or the instance settings are missing or malformed, which
writes nothing.

Instance settings (lib/config.sh): TEHAS_ALLOWED_LABELERS, the accounts an
answer may be given for.
